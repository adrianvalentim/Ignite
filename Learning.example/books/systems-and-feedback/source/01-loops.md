# Feedback Loops

This synthetic passage is only here to demonstrate the workspace format.

A feedback loop appears when a system's output later influences its own input.
The loop may amplify a change, as when confidence leads to practice and practice
increases confidence. It may also resist a change, as when a thermostat turns
heat on or off to keep a room near a target.

The important habit is to ask what returns. A list of causes is not yet a loop.
A loop exists only when influence travels back into the conditions that produced
it.
