# Systems and Feedback

## Current State

The learner has reconstructed the idea that feedback loops are patterns of
reciprocal influence, not just circular diagrams. Delays are the next point of
confusion.

## Key Insights So Far

- A reinforcing loop amplifies change.
- A balancing loop pushes a system back toward a range or target.

## Difficulties

- Remembering that delayed effects can make a stabilizing action look useless
  before it begins to work.
