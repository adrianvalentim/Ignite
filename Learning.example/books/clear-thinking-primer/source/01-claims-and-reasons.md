# Claims and Reasons

This synthetic passage is only here to demonstrate the workspace format.

A claim tells us what someone wants us to accept. A reason tells us why accepting
it would be more rational than withholding judgment. In careful reading, the
first task is to separate these two functions. A sentence can sound impressive
without actually supporting the claim beside it.

Good explanations do not merely collect facts. They show how the facts bear on
the question. When a learner can point to the claim, point to the reason, and
describe the bridge between them, the argument becomes available for testing.
