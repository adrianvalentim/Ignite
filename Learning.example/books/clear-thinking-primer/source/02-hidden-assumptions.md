# Hidden Assumptions

This synthetic passage is only here to demonstrate the workspace format.

An assumption is hidden when an argument depends on it but does not say it out
loud. Hidden assumptions are not always mistakes. They are often shared
background knowledge. The danger is that they can pass unnoticed even when they
are weak, ambiguous, or false.

To find a hidden assumption, ask what would have to be true for the reason to
support the claim. Then negate that bridge. If the argument collapses, the
assumption was doing real work.
